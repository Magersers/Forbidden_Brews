Initial CurseForge release: 0.7.2.

Sixteen potion families, 44 drinkable/splash items and 44 brewing recipes. Includes Fortune/Looting stacking, teleportation, ore highlights, flight, transformations, gravity and Netherite Wart farming.

Eleven complete language packs and an animated recipe-selection interface. Install the JAR matching your loader and Minecraft version on both client and server. Fabric also requires Fabric API.
